AMD Windows compiler dependencies

The archive includes the HIP 6.4.2 runtime compiler (hiprtc0604.dll and
hiprtc-builtins0604.dll), amd_comgr0604.dll, and ROCm device bitcode.
The compiler libraries are collected from AMD's HIP SDK 6.4.2 distribution.
Their source licenses are reproduced in hiprtc.txt, hip-headers.txt,
rocm-comgr.txt, and rocm-device-libs.txt; LLVM notices are in llvm.txt and
llvm-third-party.txt.

The AMD graphics driver provides the HIP runtime, amdhip64_6.dll, under
AMD's driver terms. That driver component is not included in this archive.
End users do not need the HIP SDK or its additional math libraries.

Deployment guidance:
https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/install-on-windows/en/docs-6.4.2/conceptual/deployment-guidelines.html
